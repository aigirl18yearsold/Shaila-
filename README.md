Project--1600 | AI SAT Question Solver

An AI-powered SAT study platform designed to help students practice, understand, and master the Digital SAT.

Features

- PDF Upload: Upload SAT question papers and practice materials.
- Camera Support: Take photos of questions directly from your device.
- Reading & Writing: Get detailed explanations of grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, and rhetorical questions.
- Mathematics: Get step-by-step mathematical solutions, calculations, and formulas.
- Bulk Question Solving: Process large question sets in manageable batches.
- Detailed Explanations: Understand why an answer is correct and why other choices are incorrect.
- Personal Notebook: Save solutions and revisit them later.
- Export: Download your saved solutions for offline reference.

- Craft and Structure

Getting Started

1. Open the website.
2. Configure your Gemini API key.
3. Upload a PDF or photograph SAT questions.
4. Select the SAT section.
5. Choose a batch size.
6. Generate detailed explanations.
7. Save or export your solutions.

Privacy and Security

- Never publish your API key in a public repository.
- Saved notebooks are stored locally in the browser.
- AI-generated answers should be checked against reliable SAT materials.

Purpose

Project--1600 aims to make high-quality SAT practice and detailed explanations more accessible to students, especially those with limited access to expensive tutoring resources.

Built for independent learners. Designed for understanding. Driven by ambition.
